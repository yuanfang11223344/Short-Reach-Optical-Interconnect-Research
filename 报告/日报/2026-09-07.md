## 📄 论文速递

1. 🔥 **arXiv:2609.01821 - "Scaling Inference Prefill with High-Radix Photonic Interconnects"。** 论文用建模方式评估 high-radix silicon photonics optical interconnect 对 MoE inference prefill 的影响，报告在高 batch、通信受限场景下改善 prefill latency 与 input-token throughput；这是 modeled evidence，不是实集群部署结果。https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01821
2. 🔥 **arXiv:2601.22585 - "HetCCL: Accelerating LLM Training with Heterogeneous GPUs"。** 论文聚焦多供应商 GPU 之间 collective communication 的性能与互操作问题；它提醒 UALink/UEC/OIF 的物理层进展需要与通信库和调度层一起验证。https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.22585
3. ⭐ **arXiv:2603.07373 - "Scheduling Parallel Optical Circuit Switches for AI Training"。** Spectra algorithm 将 AI demand matrix 分解并调度到多个 parallel OCS，同时处理 reconfiguration delay；价值在于把 OCS 从器件带宽问题落到 collective completion time。https://arxiv.org/html/2603.07373v1
4. ⭐ **arXiv:2603.21313 - "3D optoelectronics and co-packaged optics: when solving the wrong problems stalls deployment"。** 论文提醒 CPO 与 3D photonic integration 的瓶颈常在 packaging、thermal management、serviceability、standardization 与 system robustness，而不是单一器件性能。https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.21313
5. 📌 **Nature Electronics / SK hynix CPO 综述把 memory、advanced packaging 与 optical compute interconnects 放到 HPC/AI 同一系统问题中。** HBM 厂商参与 CPO 讨论，说明 AI infrastructure 的互连边界正在从 switch/optics 扩展到 memory-package-system 协同。https://news.skhynix.com/en/cpo-in-nature-electronics/
