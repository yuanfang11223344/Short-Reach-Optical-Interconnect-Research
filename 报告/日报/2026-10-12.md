3. ⭐ **Ciena Vesta 200 6.4T CPX 与 Ciena OCP 页面继续强调 scale-across connectivity for AI infrastructure。** 观察重点应放在 CPX optical engine 的可维护性、系统集成和运维接口，而不是只复述 6.4T 数字。https://www.ciena.com/insights/infobriefs/vesta-200-6-4-t-cpx-optical-engine
4. ⭐ **Lightmatter Passage L20 CPX BiDi 是 Open CPX 生态的关键样本。** 官方称其通过 bidirectional optical link technology 降低 fiber/connector 数量；后续需要看 OCP 生态、系统伙伴和客户验证证据。https://lightmatter.co/press-release/lightmatter-joins-open-cpx-msa-introduces-the-industrys-first-bidirectional-cpx-optical-engine/

## 📄 论文速递

1. 🔥 **arXiv:2608.17503 - Predict Before Replay: Joint FEC and Flight Control for Reliable Scale-Up Links。** 论文围绕 UALink 200G 1.0 replay semantics 做可靠性建模，提出 PREFACE 联合 FEC 与 outstanding-flit cap；适合作为 scale-up link reliability 背景，但仍是仿真/建模证据。https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17503
2. ⭐ **arXiv:2608.28129 - Performance Optimization of Short Reach Optical Interconnects based on Direct Detection。** 论文面向 beyond 400Gb/s per lane direct detection short-reach optical interconnect，讨论 surrogate modeling、receiver DSP optimization 与 end-to-end transceiver learning。https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28129
3. ⭐ **arXiv:2603.21313 - 3D optoelectronics and co-packaged optics。** 该文把 CPO 放在 3D integration、thermal co-design、packaging 和 standardization 的系统问题中讨论，适合补充 OCP/OIF CPO/NPO 背景。https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.21313
4. ⭐ **arXiv:2609.20686 - Volumetric Evanescent Edge Coupling。** 论文聚焦 fiber-to-chip optical I/O 的二维边缘耦合，对高密度 CPO、wafer-scale photonics 和 optical I/O packaging 有工程启发。https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20686
5. 📌 **arXiv:2608.24637 - Thermal Tuning Overhead in Wafer-Scale Optical Interconnects。** 论文提醒评估 wafer-scale optical interconnect 时要计入 thermal tuning、校准和 runtime 管理开销。https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24637
