## 📄 论文速递

1. 🔥 **arXiv:2609.01821 - "Scaling Inference Prefill with High-Radix Photonic Interconnects"。** 论文用 modeled evidence 评估 high-radix silicon photonics optical interconnect 对 MoE inference prefill 的影响，报告在高 batch、通信受限和长上下文场景中改善 prefill latency 与 input-token throughput；这不是实集群部署结果。https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01821
2. 🔥 **ACM SOSP 2026/DOI 页面对应 "Opus: Photonic Rail-Optimized Fabric in ML Datacenters"。** 论文关注 hybrid parallelism 下 optical switches 一次只能建立一对一连接的问题，并用 parallelism-driven rail reconfiguration 让不同通信阶段复用物理端口；价值在于把 photonic rails 放进训练迭代调度而不是只看器件带宽。https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3789240.3829187
3. ⭐ **arXiv:2603.07373 - "Scheduling Parallel Optical Circuit Switches for AI Training"。** Spectra algorithm 将 AI training demand matrix 分解到多个 parallel OCS，处理 reconfiguration delay 和 collective completion time，适合作为评估 OCS 是否能进入 GPU cluster fabric 的算法基线。https://arxiv.org/html/2603.07373v1
4. ⭐ **arXiv:2601.22001 - "Heterogeneous Computing: The Key to Powering the Future of AI Agent Inference"。** 论文用 Operational Intensity 和 Capacity Footprint 分析 agentic AI inference 的 memory/interconnect 瓶颈，并把 optical I/O、disaggregated serving 和 scale-up networking 放入同一系统假设；仍属研究观点，需要实测平台验证。https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.22001
5. 📌 **arXiv:2504.09892 - "Vermilion: A Traffic-Aware Reconfigurable Optical Interconnect with Formal Throughput Guarantees"。** 论文讨论 traffic-aware OCS schedule 和 worst-case throughput guarantee，适合与 Opus/Spectra 一起比较“固定周期、业务感知、训练阶段感知”三类 optical fabric 调度方法。https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.09892
