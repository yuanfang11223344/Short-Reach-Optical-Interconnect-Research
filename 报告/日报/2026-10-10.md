## 📄 论文速递

1. 🔥 **arXiv:2608.17503 - Predict Before Replay: Joint FEC and Flight Control for Reliable Scale-Up Links。** 论文基于公开 UALink 200G 1.0 replay semantics 建模，提出 PREFACE 以 corrected-symbol observation 预测 burst error，并联合选择 FEC 强度与 outstanding-flit cap；作者报告 goodput、P99 latency、replay 和 AllReduce modeling 改善。仍需注意这是仿真/建模结果，不是 silicon 或客户系统证据。https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17503
2. ⭐ **arXiv:2608.28129 - Performance Optimization of Short Reach Optical Interconnects based on Direct Detection。** 论文面向 beyond 400Gb/s per lane 的 direct-detection short-reach optical interconnect，讨论 digital surrogate modeling、receiver DSP optimization 和 end-to-end transceiver learning，可作为 400G/lane 以上短距光互连 DSP/ML 方法的背景。https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28129
3. ⭐ **arXiv:2608.11639 - Towards Terabit/λ/s Multidimensional Silicon Photonic Engine。** 论文称通过在单芯片上集成 transceiver、spatial/polarization (de)multiplexer 与 optical signal processor，探索超过 1.8 Tb/s/λ/s 的 silicon photonic engine；报告中应按实验研究处理，不写成可采购产品。https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11639
4. ⭐ **arXiv:2609.04025 - THz-SynC for Reconfigurable Hybrid Optical-THz AI Datacenters。** 论文把 THz wireless interconnect 作为 optical fabric 的补充，针对 distributed AI training 的 collective communication 进行 hybrid-fabric coordination；更适合趋势观察，不宜与现有数据中心主流部署混同。https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04025
5. 📌 **arXiv:2607.08407 - Who Needs DRAM? We Have Fiber。** 论文提出 Fiber Memory，把 optical fiber 作为 immutable data 的 recirculating delay-line memory，并结合 CPO、多芯光纤、optical regeneration 等设想；这是架构探索，适合提醒“光互连可能扩展到 memory system”，但距离产品化仍需非常谨慎。https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08407
