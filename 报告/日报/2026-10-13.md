## 🔌 SerDes与互连技术

1. 🔥 **OIF Workshop 今日在 OCP Global Summit 集中讨论 224Gbps/448Gbps electrical channel、CPO/NPO、external laser packaging、high-density electrical interconnect、coherent optics 与 CMIS firmware methodology。** 这组议题说明 400G/lane electrical I/O 的工程化已经不只是 PHY 速率问题，还要同时处理封装、供光、管理接口和现场可维护性。https://www.opencompute.org/events/ocp-summit/2026-ocp-global-summit/co-located-events/
2. 🔥 **IPEC Optical Interconnect Workshop 将 Pluggable NPO 放进 AI SuperPods/HyperPods scale-up 语境。** 官方议程强调 electrical interconnect 的物理限制、低传播损耗、高带宽和信号完整性，适合作为近封装光互连从 scale-out 走向 scale-up 的观察窗口。https://www.opencompute.org/events/ocp-summit/2026-ocp-global-summit/co-located-events/
3. ⭐ **Marvell 今日议程包含“Connectivity-Bound AI: Open Standards for Scale-Up and Scale-Out”和 SAI observability。** 这提示开放 AI fabric 的竞争会同时落在 switch/NIC、UALink-SAI、SONiC/SAI telemetry 和平台约束发现上，而不是单一芯片规格。https://www.marvell.com/company/events/ocp-global-summit-2026.html
4. ⭐ **UALink OCP panel 将讨论面向 AI workload 的 low-latency/high-bandwidth accelerator-to-switch interconnect。** 官方描述强调 interoperability、supply-chain diversity 和 deployment considerations，后续应重点核验实际系统互操作与可采购生态，而不是把标准进展等同于量产部署。https://ualinkconsortium.org/event/ocp-global-summit-2026/

## 🔦 光学与光模块

1. 🔥 **Lumentum、Astera Labs 与 Corning 在 OCP 展示 1060 nm VCSEL-based PCIe over Optics rack-level demo。** 官方称 demo 组合 Astera Scorpio Smart Fabric Switch、Lumentum 1060 nm VCSEL optical interconnect platform 与 Corning multimode fiber connectivity，用于延伸 AI rack 内 PCIe 连接；这仍应按 proof-of-concept/demo 处理，不解读为已量产系统。https://investor.lumentum.com/financial-news-releases/news-details/2026/Lumentum-Astera-Labs-and-Corning-to-Demonstrate-1060-nm-VCSEL-Based-PCIe-over-Optics-for-AI-Rack-Scale-Connectivity-at-the-2026-OCP-Global-Summit/default.aspx
2. 🔥 **该 PCIe-over-optics demo 披露了多路 32 Gb/s NRZ optical channels、Corning multimode fiber 与 error-free PCIe-5 link。** 关键意义在于 VCSEL front-end optics 正在贴近 PCIe/CXL 类 rack-scale connectivity，但指标仍限定在公开 demo 配置内，不能外推到所有 AI scale-up 协议。https://investor.lumentum.com/financial-news-releases/news-details/2026/Lumentum-Astera-Labs-and-Corning-to-Demonstrate-1060-nm-VCSEL-Based-PCIe-over-Optics-for-AI-Rack-Scale-Connectivity-at-the-2026-OCP-Global-Summit/default.aspx
3. ⭐ **Lightmatter 与 Flex 今日发布 High-Speed Open Silicon Photonics Reference Designs 议程。** 官方摘要称 test vehicle 用于验证 high-speed Open Silicon Architectures for CPO，并覆盖 passive alignment、connector/optical path testing 与 system integration；这把 CPO 风险焦点从“光引擎能否工作”推进到跨供应链参考设计和验证方法。https://lightmatter.com/event/2026-ocp-global-summit-2/
4. ⭐ **OCP Photonics track 明确覆盖 XPO、NPO、CPO、large-scale AI cluster interconnect、reliability/resiliency at scale 与 interoperability standards。** 这说明光学路线正在围绕可服务性、标准化和系统级可靠性重新排序，而不是只追求更高端口速率。https://www.opencompute.org/news/explore-22-tracks-at-the-2026-ocp-global-summit/

## 📄 论文速递

1. 🔥 **arXiv:2608.17503 - Predict Before Replay: Joint FEC and Flight Control for Reliable Scale-Up Links。** 论文用公开 UALink 200G 1.0 replay semantics 在 ns-3 中建模，提出 PREFACE 联合 FEC strength 与 outstanding-flit cap；可作为 OCP/UALink scale-up reliability 背景，但证据仍是建模与仿真。https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17503
2. ⭐ **arXiv:2608.28129 - Performance Optimization of Short Reach Optical Interconnects based on Direct Detection。** 论文讨论 direct-detection short-reach optical interconnect 的 surrogate modeling、receiver-side DSP optimization 与 end-to-end transceiver learning；其价值在于把接收端 DSP 与系统级优化放进同一个优化框架。https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28129
3. ⭐ **arXiv:2603.21313 - 3D optoelectronics and co-packaged optics。** 论文强调 heterogeneous integration、chiplet-based optics、thermal-aware co-design、standardization 和 serviceability，正好对应 OCP CPO/NPO 讨论中最容易被速率宣传掩盖的工程问题。https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.21313
4. ⭐ **arXiv:2603.07373 - Scheduling Parallel Optical Circuit Switches for AI Training。** 论文把 OCS 放进 AI training fabric 调度问题，适合支撑 OCP 中 optical circuit switching 与 AI fabric reconfiguration 的技术背景；实际部署仍需看控制面、收敛时间和 workload 适配。https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.07373
5. 📌 **Nature Electronics 2026 CPO roadmap 综述把 2D CPO、2.5D interposer 与 3D heterogeneous stacking 放进 HPC/AI optical compute interconnect 路线图。** 重点挑战包括 thermal management、manufacturability 和 standardization，可作为评估 CPO 产业化成熟度的基线。https://www.nature.com/articles/s41928-026-01681-6
