## 🔌 SerDes与互连技术

1. 🔥 **Hot Interconnects 2026 刚结束，议程把 AI fabric 的瓶颈从端口速率扩展到 telemetry、transport 和 distributed communication。** 官方 program 列出 high-radix photonic interconnect、Petabit-class MoE switching、MRC transport、Omnistat/Cassini telemetry、NVSHMEM 与 long-haul RDMA 等论文；对 224G/448G SerDes 产业的意义是，链路指标需要和 collective、tail latency、故障定位一起验证。https://hoti.org/2026/program.html
2. 🔥 **NVIDIA HotI keynote 继续强调 gigascale AI 的网络架构属性。** NVIDIA 页面称网络已成为 gigascale AI 的 defining architecture，目标从数十万 GPU 扩展到百万级互连 GPU；这会把 scale-up、scale-out、scale-across 的协议、DPU、telemetry 和光互连放进同一采购评估框架。https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/events/hot-chips-conference/
3. ⭐ **Synopsys 公开 3D PCIe 6.0 PHY 8-lane test chip，64 GT/s、PAM4、8 lane 最高 128 GB/s。** 该验证把 PCIe 6.0 PHY 从传统 2D die 推向 face-to-face 3D stack，面向 AI accelerator、HPC processor、data center switch、SmartNIC/DPU 与 CXL-enabled systems；短距高速互连正在进入封装和 3DIC 共同设计阶段。https://www.synopsys.com/blogs/chip-design/3d-pcie-6-0-phy-8-lane-test-chip.html
4. ⭐ **OIF current work 继续并行推进 CEI-224G-Linear、CEI-448G、EEI、CMIS、COI、NPO 与 coherent optics。** 这些项目把 electrical channel、module management、linear/retimed optics、external laser、near-package optics 和 compute optical interfaces 连成同一标准化队列。https://www.oiforum.com/technical-work/current-work/

## 🔦 光学与光模块

1. 🔥 **HotI 论文 “Demonstration of the 12.8T 8xDR8 High-Density Liquid-Cooled 64-Channel Pluggable XPO LRO Module and XPO Emulation Platform” 把 12.8T、liquid-cooled、pluggable、LRO 和 emulation platform 放在一起。** 这说明超高密度光模块已经从单模块速率展示进入热设计和系统仿真验证。https://hoti.org/2026/program.html
2. 🔥 **OIF 12.8Tb/s NPO Module Project 将 12.8T/6.4T near-package optics 定义在 200G/lane 上，并纳入 liquid-cooling thermal interface、laser source、power supply 与 optical connector。** NPO 的边界正在从“光靠近 ASIC”扩展为封装、供电、光源、散热和连接器的系统接口。https://www.oiforum.com/technical-work/current-work/
3. ⭐ **Marvell 1.6T optical DSP portfolio 把 Ara、Ara T、Ara X、Petra、Aquila M 覆盖到 scale-up、scale-out、scale-across AI connectivity。** 官方资料强调 DSP、drivers/TIAs、gearbox、telemetry、security 和 coherent-lite 组合；1.6T 竞争不再只是 DSP 单点，而是端到端光互连平台。https://investor.marvell.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1013/marvell-ushers-in-the-1-6t-era-with-expanded-optical-dsp-platform-portfolio-redefining-ai-data-center-end-to-end-connectivity
4. ⭐ **Keysight 224G test solutions 面向 1.6T optical network validation，把电接口、光接口和制造验证连起来。** 其公开方案支持 224 Gbps / 112 GBd PAM4 multimode optical development，适合 1.6T transceiver 从 R&D 到 early manufacturing readiness 的测试链路。https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260313711497/en/Keysight-Introduces-New-224G-Test-Solutions-to-Enable-1.6T-Optical-Network-Validation

## 📄 论文速递

1. 🔥 **HotI 2026: “Scaling Inference Prefill with High-Radix Photonic Interconnect”。** 题目直接把 high-radix photonic interconnect 放进 LLM inference prefill scaling，后续重点看光互连如何匹配 attention/prefill 的通信模式、batching 和尾延迟。https://hoti.org/2026/program.html
2. 🔥 **HotI 2026: “Breaking Through the Network Wall: Petabit-Class Switching for Single-Hop MoE Training at Scale”。** MoE training 的 expert routing 会制造高扇出、高突发 all-to-all traffic；petabit-class single-hop switching 若能工程化，会直接影响训练集群网络层级和拥塞控制设计。https://hoti.org/2026/program.html
3. ⭐ **HotI 2026: “The Multipath Reliable Connection (MRC) Transport”。** 该题目聚焦可靠传输、多路径和拥塞控制，适合与 UEC/UET、RoCE 演进、NCCL/RCCL 通信库和大规模 collective 一起观察。https://hoti.org/2026/program.html
4. ⭐ **arXiv:2608.03146 — Zero-change foundry compatible silicon photonics MEMS optical switch。** 论文展示 C-band broadband MEMS switch，摘要给出 >30dB extinction ratio、<1.5dB insertion loss 和约 20nW static power；工程化仍需关注阵列规模、可靠性、封装和控制面。https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03146
5. 📌 **HotOptics 2026: xSwitch、Slow-And-Wide Transceivers、pure photonic networks、open scale-across optical networks 等议题继续给 AI optical networking 提供研究素材。** 这些方向共同说明 optical fabric 正从器件/模块层进入系统调度、拓扑和跨站点网络层。https://conferences.sigcomm.org/sigcomm/2026/hotoptics/
