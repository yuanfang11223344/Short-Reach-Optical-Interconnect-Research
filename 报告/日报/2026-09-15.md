## 🔦 光学与光模块

1. 🔥 **OIF 在 ECOC 2026 预告多厂商 demo 和专家 session，将 448G/224G electrical、800ZR/1600ZR coherent、CMIS、CPO/NPO 和 EEI 放在同一条 AI-era interoperability 主线。** 这对 1.6T/3.2T 代际迁移很重要：真正的风险常在模块管理、线缆/板级通道、FEC、热插拔和仪表一致性。https://www.oiforum.com/oif-validates-ai-era-interoperability-live-at-ecoc-2026-through-multi-vendor-demonstrations-and-expert-sessions/
2. 🔥 **Marvell AI Infra Summit 展示清单把 Photonic Fabric、RELIANT telemetry 与 Teralynx T100 102 Tbps Ethernet switch 放在一起。** 官方材料称 Photonic Fabric 用于跨多机架 memory sharing，RELIANT 用于 optical/electrical links 可见性；这说明 vendor narrative 正在从“单芯片吞吐”转向跨 rack 的 memory/connectivity observability。https://investor.marvell.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1032/marvell-to-showcase-end-to-end-ai-data-center-connectivity-portfolio-at-ai-infra-summit-2026
3. ⭐ **ECOC 2026 展商新闻继续集中出现 CPO、224G 测试、1Tbps microLED LightBundle 等光互连主题。** 这类展前材料适合识别候选技术方向，但不能直接视为量产或大客户部署证据。https://www.ecocexhibition.com/exhibitor-news/optical-interconnect-solutions-for-cpo/
4. 📌 **1.6T optical module 叙事正在被多种形态混用：native 1.6T port、2x800G module、ASIC port capability、CPO/NPO optical engine 都可能被简称为 1.6T。** 报告引用时需要始终注明 form factor、lane rate 和测试场景，避免把展台 demo 写成规模量产。https://www.vitextech.com/blogs/blog/1-6t-optical-for-ai-data-centers-the-complete-technical-guide

## 📄 论文速递

1. 🔥 **arXiv:2609.11649 - "From Grid to Chip: Power Architecture, Stability, and Flexibility of AI Data Centers"。** 论文把 AI data centers 视为 grid-interactive computing systems，梳理 medium-voltage interface、higher-voltage DC distribution、solid-state transformers、chip-level power delivery、liquid cooling 与 stability framework；适合作为互连报告中的 power/cooling 边界条件。https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11649
2. 🔥 **arXiv:2609.04025 - "THz-SynC: Collective Synthesis with Contextual-Bandit-Assisted Coordination for Reconfigurable Hybrid Optical-THz AI Datacenters"。** 论文将 THz rack-to-rack wireless interconnect 作为 optical fabric 的补充，并围绕 All-to-All/AllReduce 优化 delay-energy tradeoff；属于 trace-driven evaluation，不等同于真实数据中心部署。https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04025
3. ⭐ **arXiv:2609.03125 - "A Time-Encoded Analog Photonic Interposer for Energy-Efficient Integration of Analog Vision Sensors and Analog Accelerators"。** 论文提出 time-encoded analog photonic interposer，用 WDM photonic link 在 chiplet 间传输模拟信号；它更偏 analog sensor/accelerator integration，不能直接外推到大模型 GPU fabric。https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03125
4. ⭐ **arXiv:2603.21313 - "3D optoelectronics and co-packaged optics: when solving the wrong problems stalls deployment"。** 论文强调 CPO 不是单器件优化，而是 packaging、thermal、serviceability、standardization 和 system architecture 的共同承诺；适合校准近期 CPO 展示的工程风险。https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.21313
5. 📌 **arXiv:2602.08284 - "2.5D co-packaged optical I/O chipsets on a SiON/Si interposer for 4 x 100G optical interconnection"。** 论文展示 SiON/Si interposer、InP active devices 与 WDM routing 的 400G single-fiber optical transceiver 原型；可作为 2.5D optical I/O 封装样本。https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.08284
