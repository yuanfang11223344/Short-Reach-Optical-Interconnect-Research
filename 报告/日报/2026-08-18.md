## 🔌 SerDes与互连技术

1. 🔥 **Hot Interconnects 2026 明天开幕，议程把 scale-up、scale-out、scale-across 放到同一互连框架。** 公开 highlights 列出 NVIDIA、Meta、Ciena keynote，以及 Napatech、Broadcom、Cisco、Eliyan、Qualcomm 等 sponsor talks；互连讨论将覆盖 programmable fabrics、distributed computing、Ethernet、SerDes 演进、chiplets 与 CPO。https://hoti.org/2026/highlights.html
2. 🔥 **NVIDIA keynote “Networking Innovations for Gigascale AI Systems” 将网络定义为 gigascale AI 的关键架构。** NVIDIA Hot Chips/HotI 页面称网络正成为 gigascale AI 的 defining architecture，目标场景从数十万 GPU 走向百万 GPU，说明互连评估已从端口速率转向系统级性能、遥测和拥塞控制。https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/events/hot-chips-conference/
3. ⭐ **Eliyan HotI 议题 “From Long Reach to Smart Reach” 指向 AI infrastructure 的 SerDes reach 重新分层。** 这类讨论的价值在于把 long reach、short reach、smart reach 与 chiplet/CPO 连接起来，提醒系统设计不能只按传统 PCB trace 或 cable reach 分类。https://hoti.org/2026/highlights.html
4. ⭐ **OIF current work 继续把 CEI-224G-Linear、CEI-448G、CMIS、EEI、NPO/COI 放在 AI 互连路线图内。** 对 1.6T/3.2T 时代，SerDes、module management、linear optics、external laser 与 co-packaging 需要一起验证，否则很难形成可部署的多厂商生态。https://www.oiforum.com/technical-work/current-work/
5. 📌 **IEEE P802.3dj 仍是 200G/lane 与 1.6TbE 收敛的核心公开入口。** 近期日报继续以 electrical/optical/logic ad hoc 资料作为参考，重点跟踪 224G electrical interface、optical PMD、FEC、link training 与 reference equalizer。https://www.ieee802.org/3/dj/public/adhoc/electrical/index.html

## 📄 论文速递

1. 🔥 **arXiv:2608.03146 — Zero-change foundry compatible silicon photonics MEMS optical switch。** 论文展示 C-band broadband silicon photonics MEMS switch，摘要给出 >30dB extinction ratio、<1.5dB insertion loss 和约 20nW static power；其关键价值是把 OCS 所需的低损耗开关与 foundry-compatible process、BEOL post-processing 结合。https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03146
2. 🔥 **HotOptics 2026 — xSwitch: An Adaptive O-E-integrated Interconnect for Scale-up Networks。** 该论文进入 “Scale-Up: Optical Interconnects Inside AI Systems” session，说明 optical-electrical integrated interconnect 正被用于探索 rack/scale-up AI 网络的自适应架构。https://conferences.sigcomm.org/sigcomm/2026/hotoptics/
3. ⭐ **HotOptics 2026 — Slow-And-Wide Transceivers Shouldn't Be Color Blind。** 题目指向 wavelength/color 与 transceiver 系统设计，适合继续跟踪是否能降低大规模 optical fabric 的拓扑、调度或功耗压力。https://conferences.sigcomm.org/sigcomm/2026/hotoptics/
4. ⭐ **ACM SIGCOMM/HotOptics — Opus: Photonic Rail-Optimized Fabric in ML Datacenters。** 论文提出用 optical circuit switches 重新实现 rail abstraction，重点在 ML 训练的通信模式、重构机会和 fabric scheduling。https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3789240.3829187
5. 📌 **HotOptics 2026 — A YANG-Grounded LLM Agent Supporting Multi-Vendor OpenROADM Optical Transport Networks。** 该议题把 LLM agent、YANG model 与多厂商 optical transport 连接起来，提示 AI 网络运维工具也会影响 scale-across 光网络的可部署性。https://conferences.sigcomm.org/sigcomm/2026/hotoptics/
