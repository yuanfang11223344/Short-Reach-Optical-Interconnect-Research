## 📄 论文速递

1. 🔥 **arXiv:2608.17503 - Predict Before Replay: Joint FEC and Flight Control for Reliable Scale-Up Links。** 论文基于公开 UALink 200G 1.0 replay semantics 建模，提出 PREFACE 以 corrected-symbol observation 预测 burst error，并联合选择 FEC 强度与 outstanding-flit cap；这是仿真/建模结果，不是 silicon 或客户系统证据。https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17503
2. ⭐ **arXiv:2608.28129 - Performance Optimization of Short Reach Optical Interconnects based on Direct Detection。** 论文讨论 beyond 400Gb/s per lane direct-detection short-reach optical interconnect、receiver DSP optimization 和 end-to-end transceiver learning。https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28129
3. ⭐ **arXiv:2608.11639 - Towards Terabit/λ/s Multidimensional Silicon Photonic Engine。** 论文探索集成 transceiver、spatial/polarization (de)multiplexer 与 optical signal processor 的 silicon photonic engine；应按实验研究处理。https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11639
4. ⭐ **arXiv:2609.04025 - THz-SynC for Reconfigurable Hybrid Optical-THz AI Datacenters。** 论文把 THz wireless interconnect 作为 optical fabric 的补充，面向 distributed AI training collective communication 做 hybrid-fabric coordination；适合趋势观察，不宜与主流部署混同。https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04025
5. 📌 **arXiv:2607.08407 - Who Needs DRAM? We Have Fiber。** 论文提出 Fiber Memory，把 optical fiber 作为 immutable data 的 recirculating delay-line memory；这是架构探索，距离产品化仍需谨慎。https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08407
