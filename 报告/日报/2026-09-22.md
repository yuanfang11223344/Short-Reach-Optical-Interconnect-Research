## 📄 论文速递

1. 🔥 **arXiv:2609.24108 - "Hypervectors from Optical Disorder: Programmable Encoding and Optical Inference for Hyperdimensional Computing"。** 论文用 silicon photonic circuit 和 scattering medium 生成 high-dimensional hypervectors，并演示 optically assisted inference path；它更偏 optical computing/photonic inference，但对 AI 基础设施的启发在于随机性、memory-compute tradeoff 与片上光计算可以结合。https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24108
2. 🔥 **arXiv:2609.21830 - "Performance of Capacitively Loaded RF Electrodes on SiO2/Si Substrates for High-Speed Electro-Optic Modulators"。** 论文实验研究 thermally grown SiO2/high-resistivity silicon 上的 traveling-wave RF electrodes，摘要称 capacitively loaded electrodes 可实现约 0.23 dB/cm/sqrt(GHz) 的 RF attenuation，并可用于 TFLN/TFLT electro-optic modulators 以及 co-packaged optical systems 中的 high-speed RF interconnects。https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21830
3. ⭐ **arXiv:2609.20686 - "Volumetric Evanescent Edge Coupling for Fiber-to-Chip Optical I/O"。** 论文提出 volumetric edge coupling，把 fiber-to-chip optical I/O 从 chip facet 单行通道扩展到二维耦合区域；摘要报告 1550 nm 仿真峰值耦合效率 88%（-0.56 dB）和 86.45 nm 1-dB bandwidth，是 CPO/optical I/O 封装的重要研究线索。https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20686
4. ⭐ **arXiv:2609.23599 - "Cryogenic thermo-optic response of low-loss phase change material for non-volatile photonic phase shifter"。** 论文在 foundry silicon photonic platform 上测量 Sb2Se3 从 4 K 到 300 K 的 cryogenic optical characteristics，面向 low-loss non-volatile photonic memory elements；它与短距互连不是直接产品线，但对 cryogenic information processing 与可编程 photonics 有参考价值。https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23599
5. 📌 **arXiv:2607.24221 - "DICE: Detailed Inter-Chiplet End-to-End PHY Modeling for Accurate Chiplet Simulation"。** 论文指出固定延迟的 chiplet link 模型会忽略 channel condition、QC-LDPC、PAM4、demodulation、retransmission 和 flow control 等动态 PHY 行为；这对 chiplet-scale SerDes 仿真和 AI accelerator partitioning 有直接方法论意义。https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24221
